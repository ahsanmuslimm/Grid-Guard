Section 01 — The Problem

The Pain Point

Energy infrastructure — power grids, oil pipelines, water treatment facilities — is the backbone of civilization. It is also the most attacked critical sector on the planet. What changed in the last 5 years is not just the volume of attacks but the nature of them. Attackers now use AI for reconnaissance, lateral movement, and evasion. Defenders are still using manual monitoring and legacy SIEM tools designed for IT — not OT.

The core problem is a three-sided gap: attacks are faster, infrastructure is more connected, and defenders have less budget and fewer AI-native tools than ever. This is not a theoretical risk. It is a documented, verified, escalating global crisis.

4×
Increase in weekly cyberattacks on utilities since 2020 (Tech Monitor / PV Magazine, 2025)
10%
Of all global cyber incidents now target the energy sector — ranked #4 most attacked (ENISA 2025)
67%
Increase in SCADA/OT vulnerability points since 2021 due to IT/OT convergence (Market Growth Reports)
68%

Pakistan's 2023 national blackout caused over PKR 100 billion in economic damage in a single event. The root cause was a cascading grid failure — the exact scenario a targeted cyberattack would deliberately engineer. Similar events have hit Poland (December 2025), Spain, Portugal, and Eastern Europe (2025). This is not a future threat. It is a present operational reality.

The specific gap GridGuard targets: there is no affordable, AI-native, explainable autonomous response system for OT/SCADA environments. Enterprise solutions like Claroty or Dragos cost $500K+ annually. Emerging market energy operators, municipal grids, and mid-size utilities globally have nothing.

Section 02 — The Solution

What GridGuard Is

GridGuard is an autonomous multi-step AI agent built on Google Cloud Agent Builder and powered by Gemini, that detects, investigates, and responds to cyber threats targeting energy infrastructure — with complete observability and explainability of every AI decision via Arize Phoenix.

The critical differentiator is not just detection. It is trust. When an AI agent makes a security decision — isolate this node, block this IP, escalate this alert — an energy operator needs to know: why did it decide that? Was the reasoning sound? Did it hallucinate? Arize Phoenix answers exactly these questions. GridGuard is the first system to combine autonomous OT threat response with LLM-level observability and evaluation.

One Line Pitch
  • Energy grids are being attacked by AI. The AI defending them can't be trusted or explained. GridGuard fixes both.
Section 03 — Agent Workflow

How The Agent Works

GridGuard operates as a 6-step autonomous mission pipeline. Each step is traced, evaluated, and logged by Arize Phoenix in real time.

01
Trigger
Ingests SCADA telemetry + public threat intel feeds. Detects anomalous patterns.
02
Investigate
Agent queries multiple sources. Correlates signatures. Determines real threat vs false positive.
03
Decide
Classifies severity. Selects response playbook. Routes Critical to human approval gate.
04
Respond
Executes automated response: isolate node, block IP, alert operator, generate ticket.
05
Explain
Generates plain-English incident report. Shows reasoning chain for every decision.
06
Learn
Arize evaluates response quality. Flags hallucinations. Feeds back into improvement loop.
